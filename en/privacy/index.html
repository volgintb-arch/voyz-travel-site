Privacy policy

A short version for the request form to work. Before the site launches it must be reviewed by a lawyer and completed with the company’s details.

What data we collect

Name, contact details (Telegram, WhatsApp, phone or email), preferred travel season, number of travelers and a comment — only what you yourself provide in the request form.

Why

To contact you, answer your questions and prepare the tour program and quote.

Who receives the data

We do not sell or pass your data to third parties, except where it is needed to organize the trip (for example, to arrange permits for the border zone) and only with your consent.

How to withdraw consent

Write to us in any convenient way — we will delete your data.
