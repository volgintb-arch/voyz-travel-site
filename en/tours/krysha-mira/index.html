Who it’s for

This trip is not for everyone

It is important to understand honestly what awaits you: roads, altitude, overnight conditions and weather.

Drives

Long days on mountain roads and passes

Physical effort

Walks at altitude, effort is optional

Altitude

Base camp at about 3,600 m

Comfort

Yurts and simple guesthouses

This is one of our highest-altitude routes. Before booking we will discuss health and acclimatization.

It suits you if

  • You are drawn to big mountains, glaciers and open sky
  • You are comfortable with altitude up to 3,600 m and long roads
  • The view and the silence matter more to you than comfort

Better to choose something else if

  • You tolerate altitude poorly
  • You expect a hotel with service every day
  • You are not ready for cold nights in the mountains

Questions

What people usually ask

Didn’t find your question? Write to us, we will answer and tell you whether this route suits you.

Ask a question →
Is this a tour for mountaineers?

No. We do not climb Lenin Peak: we drive to its foot in 4x4 vehicles and walk around the base camp.

How do I avoid altitude sickness?

The route is built with a gradual gain in altitude and a day to adjust. If you have health questions, discuss them with a doctor before the trip.

How much does the trip cost?

The price depends on the size of the group, the dates and the services you choose, so we do not publish a single figure “for everyone”. Leave a request and we will send a program with a quote.

What if the weather turns bad?

The program may change due to weather, road conditions and participants’ safety. In the mountains that is normal: we discuss backup options in advance.

Can I travel with my own group?

Yes. We can put together a group for you: friends, family or colleagues. Leave a request and tell us how many of you there are.
