Highlights

What you will see

The medieval caravanserai of Tash-Rabat, the vast Lake Chatyr-Kul and evenings at Song-Kul: the path of nomads and merchants by 4x4.

caravanserai

Tash-Rabat

A stone caravanserai in a valley where the merchants of the Great Silk Road once stopped.

high-altitude lake

Chatyr-Kul

A huge lake at more than 3,000 meters among open steppes. The area is in a border zone.

yurts and jailoo

Song-Kul

Final evenings in yurts by the water and herds of horses at sunset.

Program

6 days in order

We do not tie a day to the minute: in the mountains the rhythm is set by weather and road. But we know exactly where you will be and what you will see.

Naryn
01

Day 1

South, deep into the Tian Shan

Bishkek → Kochkor → Naryn

We leave Bishkek and climb into the mountains: passes, valleys, the first herds. In the evening — Naryn, where the real backcountry begins.

  • Mountain passes
  • Kochkor
  • Naryn
OvernightNaryn, hotel
Tash-Rabat
02

Day 2

To the stone walls of the caravans

Naryn → At-Bashy → Tash-Rabat

The road along the At-Bashy river leads to Tash-Rabat, a medieval caravanserai among the hills. In the evening you can walk through the valley and hear the silence in which caravans once rested.

  • Tash-Rabat caravanserai
  • The valley in the evening
  • Yurt camp
OvernightTash-Rabat, yurt camp
Lake Chatyr-Kul
03

Day 3

A lake on the roof of the world

Tash-Rabat → Chatyr-Kul → At-Bashy

We drive to Chatyr-Kul: a huge expanse of water among open ranges. Lots of sky here and few people. We return to At-Bashy for dinner in a guesthouse.

  • High-altitude steppes
  • Lake Chatyr-Kul
  • Dinner in a guesthouse

The area is in a border zone: permits are arranged in advance.

OvernightAt-Bashy, guesthouse

  • Sunset by the water

Who it’s for

This trip is not for everyone

It is important to understand honestly what awaits you: roads, altitude, overnight conditions and weather.

Drives

Long sections on mountain and dirt roads

Physical effort

Walks near the caravanserai and on the jailoo

Altitude

Chatyr-Kul and Song-Kul above 3,000 m

Comfort

Yurts and simple guesthouses

The scale is approximate. Altitude is a factor on several days of the route: we will discuss how you feel and your preparation in advance.

It suits you if

  • You are interested in the history and atmosphere of the Silk Road, not just the views
  • You enjoy silence, open sky and unhurried days
  • You are ready for simple conditions in yurts and guesthouses

Better to choose something else if

  • You expect hotel service every day
  • You want packed guided tours of museums
  • You dislike long roads and altitude

Questions

What people usually ask

Didn’t find your question? Write to us, we will answer and tell you whether this route suits you.

Ask a question →
What makes Tash-Rabat interesting?

It is a medieval stone caravanserai in a mountain valley. It is easy to imagine how merchants rested on the Great Silk Road.

Do I need permits?

For the Chatyr-Kul area, yes: it is in a border zone, and permits are arranged in advance.

How much does the trip cost?

The price depends on the size of the group, the dates and the services you choose, so we do not publish a single figure “for everyone”. Leave a request and we will send a program with a quote.

What if the weather turns bad?

The program may change due to weather, road conditions and participants’ safety. In the mountains that is normal: we discuss backup options in advance.

Can I travel with my own group?

Yes. We can put together a group for you: friends, family or colleagues. Leave a request and tell us how many of you there are.
