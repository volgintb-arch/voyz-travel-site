Program

7 days in order

We do not tie a day to the minute: in the mountains the rhythm is set by weather and road. But we know exactly where you will be and what you will see.

Kok-Moynok canyons
01

Day 1

From another planet to the big mountains

Bishkek → Kok-Moinok → Issyk-Kul → Karakol

We start among the red Kok-Moinok canyons, a landscape from another planet. Then the road along Issyk-Kul, mountain ranges and the first conversations in our group. By evening — Karakol.

  • Kok-Moynok canyons
  • Issyk-Kul shore
  • Karakol
OvernightKarakol
Altyn-Arashan valley
02

Day 2

Where the real mountains begin

Karakol → Altyn-Arashan

We leave familiar roads behind and climb into the Altyn-Arashan mountain valley. Conifer forests, mountain rivers, natural hot springs — and an evening among the peaks, far from the city.

  • Altyn-Arashan valley
  • Hot springs
  • An evening in the mountains
OvernightAltyn-Arashan valley
Jeti-Oguz gorge
03

Day 3

The valley of red rocks

Jeti-Oguz → Maiden's Braids → Seven Bulls → Broken Heart

We descend from the mountains into the Jeti-Oguz gorge: red rocks, spruce forests and green valleys in one frame. On horseback we reach the Maiden's Braids waterfall, have lunch in the mountains and in the evening drive to the south shore of Issyk-Kul.

  • Seven Bulls rocks
  • On horseback to the waterfall
  • Lunch in the mountains
OvernightSouth shore of Issyk-Kul
Skazka canyon
04

Day 4

Waterfalls and Skazka canyon

Barskoon → Skazka canyon

First the green Barskoon gorge and the sound of waterfalls. Then Skazka canyon: red and golden rocks that look like a frozen city.

  • Barskoon waterfalls
  • Skazka canyon
  • South shore of Issyk-Kul
“33 Parrots” serpentine
05

Day 5

Road to the Nomads

Ak-Sai → 33 Parrots pass → Son-Kul

We say goodbye to Issyk-Kul and walk among the unusual Ak-Sai rocks. Then the road climbs into the mountains: the 33 Parrots serpentine, stops for the views, and in the evening a yurt camp and the first night on Son-Kul.

  • Ak-Sai rocks
  • 33 Parrots pass
  • First night on Son-Kul
OvernightSong-Kul, yurt camp
Song-Kul
06

Day 6

A whole day on the jailoo

Song-Kul

We wake up by the lake and don't pack. We walk across high pastures, watch life on the jailoo or simply sit by the water. In the evening — a shared table, conversations and a second night on Son-Kul.

  • Morning by the lake
  • Life on the jailoo
  • An evening at a shared table
OvernightSong-Kul, yurt camp
Kalmak-Ashuu pass
07

Day 7

One more morning by the lake

Song-Kul → Kalmak-Ashuu → Bishkek

A last breakfast on Son-Kul and a few quiet minutes by the water. We return over the Kalmak-Ashuu pass: a different road and new views. Back in Bishkek with the feeling of a big journey.

  • Breakfast on Son-Kul
  • Kalmak-Ashuu pass
  • Return to Bishkek
Who it’s for

This trip is not for everyone

It is important to understand honestly what awaits you: roads, altitude, overnight conditions and weather.

Drives

Every day on the road, the 33 Parrots and Kalmak-Ashuu passes

Physical effort

Walks and short horse rides

Altitude

Two nights on Son-Kul, ≈ 3000 m

Comfort

Guesthouses and a yurt camp

The scale is approximate. If the road to Altyn-Arashan does not allow us up, we will spend the day in Karakol: a cooking masterclass and the Ak-Suu hot springs.

It suits you if

  • You want to feel nomadic Kyrgyzstan, not just see it through a window
  • You dream of waking up by a high-altitude lake with nowhere to rush
  • You are ready for mountain roads and simple conditions in a yurt camp

Better to choose something else if

  • You need hotel comfort every night: on Son-Kul we stay in yurts
  • You do not tolerate altitude around 3000 m well
  • You are not ready for long drives on mountain roads

Ask a question →
How much does the trip cost?

110,000 KGS per person. The price includes a hotel night in Bishkek before the start and breakfasts; other meals are paid on the spot.

When can I go?

In 2027: May 26 – June 1, June 3–9, June 11–17 and June 19–25.

How many people are in the group?

6–8 travellers and two 4x4s.

What about altitude?

Son-Kul lies at about 3000 m. We climb gradually, and if you have health questions we will discuss them in advance.

What if the weather turns bad?

The program may change due to weather, road conditions and participants’ safety. In the mountains that is normal: we discuss backup options in advance.

Can I travel with my own group?

Yes. We can put together a group for you: friends, family or colleagues. Leave a request and tell us how many of you there are.
