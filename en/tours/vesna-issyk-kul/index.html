Highlights

What you will see

In spring the mountains come alive while tourists are still few: the red rocks of Skazka and Jeti-Oguz, Barskoon gorge and a calm Issyk-Kul.

Who it’s for

This trip is not for everyone

It is important to understand honestly what awaits you: roads, altitude, overnight conditions and weather.

Drives

Short days, a calm pace

Physical effort

Easy walks

Altitude

Low and medium altitudes

Comfort

Hotels and guesthouses

The easiest and calmest route. Good for getting to know Kyrgyzstan.

It suits you if

  • You want to see the main places of Issyk-Kul without the summer crowds
  • You like soft light, fresh greenery and silence
  • You accept changeable weather at the start of the season

Better to choose something else if

  • You want warmth and swimming in the lake
  • You need guaranteed clear weather
  • You dislike possible snow and mud on the trails

Questions

What people usually ask

Didn’t find your question? Write to us, we will answer and tell you whether this route suits you.

Ask a question →
What is the weather like in spring?

Days can be warm and sunny, or cool and rainy. We choose the program so that it can be changed flexibly.

Can I swim?

No, the lake is cold in spring. This tour is about canyons, mountains and silence.

How much does the trip cost?

The price depends on the size of the group, the dates and the services you choose, so we do not publish a single figure “for everyone”. Leave a request and we will send a program with a quote.

What if the weather turns bad?

The program may change due to weather, road conditions and participants’ safety. In the mountains that is normal: we discuss backup options in advance.

Can I travel with my own group?

Yes. We can put together a group for you: friends, family or colleagues. Leave a request and tell us how many of you there are.
